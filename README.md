# Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Summary
This project analyzes Amazon product sales performance using an interactive dashboard. It tracks key performance indicators (KPIs), including YTD Sales, QTD Sales, Products Sold, and Customer Reviews. The dashboard visualizes monthly and weekly sales trends, product category performance, and the top 5 products by sales and reviews. Interactive filters allow users to explore data by product category and quarter, helping identify sales trends, top-performing products, and opportunities for business growth.

## Features
- KPI cards for YTD sales, QTD sales, products sold, and reviews
- Monthly sales line chart and weekly sales column chart
- Product-category sales table
- Top 5 products by YTD sales and by YTD reviews
- Sidebar category and quarter filters
- Dark dashboard styling
